# Inputs for MONET

* *ICCT_MONET_2035.csv*: EV Sales projections for 2035 by country, from the ICCT Roadmap v2.2. 
* *ag.csv*: Region and country classification.
* *batsize.csv*: Average battery capacity, in kWh, for each region and market segment. Aggregated data from EV Volumes.
* *sales_share_s1.csv*: Calculated trade ratios matrix for sales in market segment 1.
* *sales_share_s2.csv*: Calculated trade ratios matrix for sales in market segment 2.
* *sales_share_s3.csv*: Calculated trade ratios matrix for sales in market segment 3.
* *sales_share_s4.csv*: Calculated trade ratios matrix for sales in market segment 4.
* *segment_sales_share - MONET.csv*: Sales share among market segments for each country.
* *supplystats_seg.csv*: Estimated trade results for each country after balancing; s: sales, p: production, ds: domestic sales, s.ds: Share of domestic sales, fs: foreign supply.
* *LDV_export_share.csv*: LDV Export shares of global market, input used as parameter for Global Free Trade Scenario
